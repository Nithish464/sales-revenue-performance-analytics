# Data Dictionary

`order_id` unique synthetic order; `order_date` date; `city`/`region` geography; `category`/`product` product hierarchy; `channel` acquisition channel; `customer_segment` synthetic segment; `quantity` units; `unit_price` price per unit; `gross_sales` pre-discount sales; `discount_amount` and `discount_pct`; `net_sales`; `cogs`; `shipping_cost`; `marketing_cost`; `profit`; `order_status`; `customer_rating`; `payment_method`.
